# Drafting Branch

Draft new prose from an evidence spine and verified source material.

## 1. Freeze the local contract

Identify:

- The requested section and its job in the paper.
- The claim it must advance or close.
- The facts, citations, figures, and tables it may use.
- The concepts already defined and the reader state at entry.
- The target length and surrounding LaTeX conventions.

If no plan exists, form a lightweight local outline. Do not silently invent the whole-paper narrative.

Complete this step when the paragraph or section has a bounded job and a bounded evidence set.

## 2. Choose section moves

Read the matching section in [systems-rhetoric.md](systems-rhetoric.md). Select only the moves needed for the local contract.

Typical paragraph structure:

```text
claim -> reason or mechanism -> evidence -> interpretation -> transition
```

Vary the structure when a definition, example, limitation, or design alternative must lead. Avoid throat-clearing openers and section narration that delays the claim.

Complete this step when each planned paragraph has one primary function.

## 3. Draft against sources

- Introduce a concept before relying on it.
- Tie each design choice to a requirement, observation, or rejected alternative.
- State evaluation questions before presenting answers.
- Report absolute context alongside relative improvements when available.
- Interpret results without repeating every plotted value.
- Preserve unfavorable results and conditions that bound generality.
- Use calibrated novelty language; write `to our knowledge` only after a current literature check.

Do not fill evidence gaps with plausible prose. Use a visible placeholder or narrow the claim.

Complete this step when every sentence either advances the local contract or supplies information necessary to understand it.

## 4. Integrate the paper

Check:

- Terminology and symbols match surrounding sections.
- Cross-references and citation keys exist.
- Figure captions and nearby prose agree on the takeaway.
- Contribution wording does not drift from the introduction or plan.
- The section starts from the reader's current state and ends at the next section's required state.

Complete this step when the prose can be inserted without creating a definition, reference, or narrative discontinuity.

## 5. Run drafting validation

Trace every factual and quantitative sentence to its source. Recheck numbers, units, modality, causal language, and scope. Resolve unsupported statements or mark them explicitly.

Complete this step only when all sentences are grounded or visibly unresolved.

## Output contract

Return the requested prose in the repository's format. Do not attach a generic critique. Add `Open items` only for correctness gaps that the user must resolve.
