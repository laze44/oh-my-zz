# HPCA-style review reference

Adapted from the user-supplied **HPCA 2027 Review Form** on 2026-09-09, without its example reviewer or paper identity. This is a framework for an author-facing review draft, not an uploadable form or another venue's policy.

## Assessment dimensions

Summarize the problem, contribution, mechanism, and evidence faithfully. Identify the paper's important strengths and weaknesses, then develop the decisive reasoning in comments for authors. Consider contribution significance, design feasibility, evaluation credibility, and the scope of the conclusions as relevant to the work. Let the paper's claims determine the necessary scrutiny rather than requiring a standard list of experiments.

Use these scales when giving scores, and justify the assessment:

| Dimension | Scale |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-Rebuttal Overall merit | 1 Reject: fundamental flaws; 2 Weak reject: merits below acceptance; 3 Weak accept: merits outweigh weaknesses; 4 Accept: strong contribution, minor issues; 5 Strong accept: outstanding contribution. |
| Soundness | 1 Central claims invalidated by fundamental errors; 2 Major gaps leave central claims unconvincing; 3 Main claims supported with minor gaps; 4 Rigorous and thorough support. |
| Novelty and Expected Impact | 1 Neither novel nor impactful; 2 Limited novelty or impact; 3 Meaningfully new and relevant contribution; 4 Important new direction or substantial change in understanding. |
| Fit For HPCA | 1 Traditional scope; 2 Non-traditional but interesting to HPCA; 3 Non-traditional with limited HPCA interest; 4 Out of scope. Apply only when HPCA is the target; otherwise discuss the relevant venue qualitatively. |

Explain novelty and impact separately even though the form combines them. Paper-only review cannot establish literature-wide originality. Overall merit is a judgment about the central contribution and evidence, not an average of other scores or a count of language errors.

Comments should explain why concerns matter and offer feasible revisions. Rebuttal questions should focus on uncertainties whose answers could change the assessment; distinguish those from substantial new work for revision.

## Actual form preparation, only when requested

Personal fields—previous review history, reviewer expertise, intellectual conflict, LLM-use attestations, and ethics declarations—require the actual reviewer's answers. Leave unknown values pending; report analytic confidence separately from personal research credentials. AI assistance cannot truthfully select “no LLM use” or certify the form's compound LLM declaration on the reviewer's behalf.

The supplied form hides venue fit, review history, PC comments, LLM use, and intellectual conflict from authors; ethics fields are administrator-only. Preserve those visibility distinctions in form drafts, while keeping essential technical criticism in author-facing comments. Default coaching reports need not include private fields.

When filling a supplied offline form, edit a copy and preserve `==+==` and `==*==` lines exactly. Do not copy identities from examples, mark it Ready, or submit it automatically.
