# ASPLOS 2027 reference

Source: [ASPLOS 2027 Call for Papers](https://www.asplos-conference.org/asplos2027/cfp/), consulted 2026-09-09. This dated summary is an example, not a universal architecture-conference policy.

- Assess relevance, originality, technical quality, and clarity. Contributions may advance architecture, operating systems, or programming languages; a new domain must meaningfully connect to a core area. Useful new understanding of existing systems can qualify.
- Examine problem significance, practical reasoning, experimental credibility, disadvantages as well as benefits, and explicit separation of implemented and unimplemented work.
- The rapid round reads only the first two pages. For that target, assess whether those pages convey the problem and research advance. This skill still reviews the whole paper unless explicitly asked for rapid-round scope.
- Regular submissions allow 11 main-text pages; references, appendices, and the specified AI-use acknowledgment are excluded. Main-text claims must stand on their own. Revision/camera-ready allowances differ.
- Submission uses PDF and `acmart` with `sigplan,anonymous,review,nonacm`, 10-point body text, numbered pages, and no space squeezing. Figure/table text must remain legible; below 8 points is forbidden.
- Double-blind and disclosure requirements apply. Check only observable manuscript properties; unknown submission history or personal declarations remain unassessed.

When exact current compliance is requested, use a user-supplied current policy or explicitly authorized official-policy lookup. Keep the dated snapshot label if unverified. Never apply ASPLOS page limits or rapid-round rules to HPCA or another venue automatically.
